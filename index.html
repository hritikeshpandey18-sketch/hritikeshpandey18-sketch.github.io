About

Technical operations, data, and a stage voice

I'm an M.Sc. Information Technology student at Thakur College of Science and Commerce, after completing my B.Sc. IT there in 2025. My work sits where software meets data: I design relational databases, write the Java logic around them, and build dashboards that make the numbers readable.

Before my degree, I spent almost two years running online examination centres for MeritTrac, TCS iON and Ginger. That meant keeping servers stable during high-stakes government and banking recruitment exams, and solving problems fast with hundreds of candidates waiting.

I'm drawn to Data Science and Business Intelligence, with a record of applying machine learning and analytical thinking to real-world problems such as agriculture.
